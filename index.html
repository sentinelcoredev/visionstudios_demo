10 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?+

Starter projects begin from ₹10,000, business websites from ₹20,000, and advanced projects are quoted based on requirements.

How long does a website take?+

Time depends on scope, content, features and revisions. A simple site can move much faster than a complex custom build.

Do you build mobile-friendly websites?+

Yes. Responsive behavior across mobile, tablet and desktop is part of the design process.

Can you add custom features?+

Yes, where technically and commercially practical. Custom JavaScript interactions, calculators, forms and integrations can be scoped into a project.

Do you use AI?+

Yes. AI is used openly as a development accelerator for repetitive work, implementation assistance, debugging and iteration. Human decisions remain central.

Will I be able to update my website?+

That depends on the deployment and setup. A suitable editing workflow can be discussed when the project is scoped.

Do you provide maintenance?+

Maintenance can be discussed as a separate requirement depending on what the website needs after launch.

Can you integrate WhatsApp?+

Yes, where the required WhatsApp workflow is supported by the project setup.

Can you build calculators or interactive tools?+

Yes. JavaScript can be used for calculators, estimators, dynamic UI and other browser-side interactions.
